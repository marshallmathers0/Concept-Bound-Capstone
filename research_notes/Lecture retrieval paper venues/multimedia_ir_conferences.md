## Which resource/dataset tracks suit a new annotated lecture-moment benchmark?

### Takeaway
The best-suited tracks are: **ECIR Resource** (closes too early, 2 Nov 2026); **SIGIR 2027 Resource & Reproducibility** (TBA, estimated Feb 2027); **CIKM 2027 Resource** (estimated June 2027); **ACM MM 2027 Dataset track** (if it repeats; stretch); and **ICMR 2027 regular short/long**, which welcomes benchmark and evaluation-methodology work (31 Jan 2027). For realistic odds, the safer alternatives are CBMI 2027 and ICME 2027 workshops, which have no formal resource track but welcome dataset and benchmark contributions.

### Cited Findings
- ECIR 2027 Resource: explicitly covers "Novel training datasets and test collections, or extensions of existing collections with additional labelled annotations" and annotation guidelines. Single-blind. 12 pages + references. Deadline 2 Nov 2026 (18 days, too early). — [ECIR 2027 Call for Resource Papers](https://www.ecir2027.co.uk/call-for-resource-papers)
- SIGIR runs a combined "Resource & Reproducibility" track. SIGIR 2027 lists it as a placeholder with no dates. In 2025 the deadline was 18 Feb. — [SIGIR 2027 tracks](https://sigir2027.org/pages/submit-tracks.html); [SIGIR 2025 R&R call](https://sigir2025.dei.unipd.it/call-res-repro-papers.html)
- CIKM Resource: 4 pages, single-blind. 2026 deadlines were abstract 30 May and full paper 6 Jun. — [CIKM 2026 Resource Papers](https://cikm2026.diag.uniroma1.it/resource-papers/)
- ACM MM 2026 had a dedicated "Call for Dataset Papers"; whether MM 2027 repeats it is unknown. — [ACM MM 2026 Call for Dataset Papers](https://2026.acmmm.org/site/call-datasets.html)
- ICMR 2027 topics include "Benchmarks and evaluation methodologies for multimedia analysis/search", but there is no separate resource track. Its Reproducibility track (22 Dec 2026) is for reproducing prior work, not new datasets. — [ICMR 2027 CfC](https://www.icmr2027.org/cfc); [ICMR 2027 Dates](https://www.icmr2027.org/date)

### Inferences
- A ~30-video benchmark from one institution is small for SIGIR, CIKM or ACM MM resource tracks. Reviewers there typically expect larger scale, licensing and release clarity, and inter-annotator agreement. To be competitive, the team should report:
  - annotation protocol and inter-annotator agreement
  - the number of queries and moments, not just videos
  - licensing and consent for releasing classroom recordings (a key risk for recorded lectures of identifiable instructors and students)
  - strong baselines, e.g. BM25, dense retrieval, and existing lecture-video or moment-retrieval models
- Realistic plan:
  1. Target ICMR 2027 short paper (31 Jan) as the stretch submission.
  2. Fall back to ICMR Demo (13 Feb), SIGIR 2027 Resource & Reproducibility (if dated around Feb), ICME 2027 workshops, CBMI 2027, or BEA 2027 (Mar–Apr).
  3. Keep CIKM 2027 Resource (June) as a later resource-focused option.
- Honest acceptance outlook: main tracks at ACM MM, SIGIR full, ICME main and CVPR are not realistic for this undergraduate applied paper. ICMR short and the SIGIR, CIKM or ECIR resource tracks are stretch targets. Workshops (ICME, ICMR, BEA, CVPR video or education workshops if any appear) and CBMI are the realistic tier.
