### Inferences (fit and acceptance assessment for this paper)
Ranked by realism for a student-authored applied benchmark plus empirical study, ready about mid-January 2027:

1. **IEEE ICALT 2027, Full (5 pp) or Short (3 pp). VERIFIED 15 Jan 2027 (92 days). Borderline: tight at "mid-January".**
   - Topical fit is good under the BDELA or AISLE tracks.
   - The 5-page cap including references forces a compressed paper: system, benchmark and the key ablation (concept segments vs. fixed windows, compared with the BM25 and SBERT baselines).
   - ICALT is a broad IEEE venue that regularly accepts applied system papers. The realistic acceptance chance for a sound short or full paper is moderate.
   - Proceedings are in IEEE Xplore with Scopus/EI indexing, which is useful for Indian institutional credit.
   - Recommendation: aim to finish by about 10 Jan. If it slips, look for an extension like the one in 2026, but do not count on it.
2. **ACM L@S 2027 Work-in-Progress (4 pp), ESTIMATED about 16 Feb 2027 (124 days).**
   - Lecture-video navigation at scale is squarely L@S territory, and the WiP format fits honest early results.
   - WiP acceptance is typically much more lenient than research papers. ACM DL indexing.
   - The full 10-page research track would be hard with only 30 videos from one college.
3. **AIED 2027 Late-Breaking Results, ESTIMATED about early Apr 2027 (≈172 days); main track about 2 Feb 2027 (≈110 days).**
   - LBR (Springer CCIS) is a realistic target. The main track (CORE A, ≈18% full-paper acceptance in 2025) is a reach for this paper.
   - A short main-track paper is possible if the empirical study is rigorous, especially an honest negative result showing strong text baselines matching the multimodal fusion.
4. **EDM 2027 Short (6 pp) about 9 Feb 2027 (117 days) or Poster/Demo (2-4 pp) about 6 Apr 2027 (173 days), ESTIMATED.**
   - EDM values rigorous evaluation and released datasets, so a human-annotated benchmark is a plus.
   - A demo of the retrieval system also fits.
5. **IEEE FIE 2027 WIP (≤5 pp), ESTIMATED abstract about 26 Jan 2027 (103 days).**
