## Q3. How to spot predatory "Scopus-indexed" conferences in India, and typical submission-to-acceptance timelines

### Takeaway
Verify the claim at the source, not on the conference site. Check that the IEEE conference record number exists on IEEE Conference Search and that past editions are on IEEE Xplore. For Springer, check that past volumes are on link.springer.com. Check Scopus coverage of the series at scopus.com/sources. Red flags include multi-city "conference brands" (e.g., WASET's ICCVIP 2027, run in Zurich, Dubai, London, Tokyo and Singapore), acceptance within days, and vague "Scopus-indexed" promises. Legitimate Indian venues take about 2–9 weeks from deadline to notification.

### Cited Findings
- Verification steps: search the free Scopus source list (scopus.com/sources) by series title or ISSN, and check that coverage reads "to Present" rather than ending in a past year. Confirm "submitted to IEEE Xplore" claims on IEEE's own portal. Search Scopus or Xplore for papers from the previous edition. Treat "Published by [Conference Name] Press" as a warning sign — [AIScholar verify Scopus](https://www.aischolar.com/news/article/how-to-verify-scopus-indexing); [AIScholar red flags](https://www.aischolar.com/news/article/predatory-scopus-conferences-7-red-flags-to-watch) (caveat: AIScholar is a commercial conference-listing company)
- Red flags: unsolicited flattering invitations, acceptance within 24–72 hours, a programme committee you can't verify, indexing in unrecognised databases, recently registered domains, names that imitate known conferences, multiple "conferences" at the same venue and time, and early-bird fee pressure — [AIScholar red flags](https://www.aischolar.com/news/article/predatory-scopus-conferences-7-red-flags-to-watch); [Exordo: 9 signs of a fake conference](https://www.exordo.com/blog/9-signs-this-is-a-fake-conference/)
- Example of name-spoofing: ENNS warned that WASET runs fake "conferences" for registration fees and spoofs real conference names (here ICANN) — [ENNS: Beware of fake conferences](https://e-nns.org/icann2018/index.php/2016/11/11/beware-of-fake-conferences)
- Live example relevant to this topic: "International Conference on Computer Vision and Image Processing (ICCVIP) 2027", organised by WASET, is listed in Zurich (14–15 Jan 2027), Dubai, London, Tokyo and Singapore. The listings show notification dates that come before the submission deadlines. Do NOT confuse it with India's CVIP (NIT Calicut / IAPR-style series) — [conferenceindex ICCVIP Zurich](https://conferenceindex.org/event/international-conference-on-computer-vision-and-image-processing-iccvip-2027-january-zurich-ch); [conferenceindex ICCVIP Dubai](https://conferenceindex.org/event/international-conference-on-computer-vision-and-image-processing-iccvip-2027-february-dubai-ae)
- In India, also check that the venue is on the UGC/institution-approved list, since Scopus indexing alone may not satisfy local requirements — [AIScholar](https://www.aischolar.com/news/article/how-to-verify-scopus-indexing)
- Observed deadline-to-notification gaps at legitimate venues: INDICON 2026 31 Aug → 2 Nov (~9 weeks) [IEEE India Council](https://ieeeindiacouncil.org/ieee-india-council-international-conference-indicon-2026/); CONECCT 2027 15 Dec → 15 Feb (~9 weeks) [CONECCT](https://www.ieee-conecct.org.ieeebangalore.org/); T4E 2026 26 Jul → 20 Sep (~8 weeks) [T4E](https://sites.google.com/view/t4e2026); FIRE 2026 15 Aug → 15 Oct (~9 weeks) [FIRE CFP](https://groups.google.com/g/fire-list/c/JOPgANaf6qk); CVIP 2026 R1 30 Apr → 15 Jun (~6.5 weeks) [CVIP](https://cvip2026.nitc.ac.in/); ICCUBEA 2026 10 Apr → 10 May (~4 weeks) [PCCOE](https://www.research.pccoepune.com/conferences.html); I2ITCON 2026 15 Jan → 30 Jan (~2 weeks) [myhuiban](https://www.myhuiban.com/conference/5325.md). The conference is typically 1.5–4 months after notification, and IEEE Xplore publication follows the conference after post-conference review.

### Inferences
- Practical checklist for the students:
  1. Find the IEEE conference record number on conferences.ieee.org and confirm the sponsor is an IEEE Section, Council or Society.
  2. Open the previous edition on IEEE Xplore, Springer Link or the ACM DL.
  3. Check that the committee members are real faculty (IITs, NITs, IISc, ISI, IIITs).
  4. Distrust any venue that promises acceptance in under a week, publishes "in Scopus journals" from a conference, or runs the same name in several cities.
- Because Xplore inclusion is conditional on presentation, the "publication" milestone for marks is usually the acceptance letter plus the presentation certificate. The Xplore DOI can arrive 1–3 months after the event. This is an inference from the "presented papers, subject to quality review" language and was not confirmed for any specific venue.
